THE RECRUITING AGENT

Screen on evidence, schedule without the back-and-forth.

Every score quotes the resume line it came from. Blind mode hides names and other personal details before scoring, instructions hidden in resumes are flagged and ignored, and people make every decision. Demo data is a fictional company on .example addresses; nothing is ever sent.
